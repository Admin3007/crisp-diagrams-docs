# Crisp Diagrams for Confluence

Render Mermaid diagrams as **true SVG** — crisp at any zoom, full page width, with pan, zoom and
full screen. No account. No uploads. Nothing leaves Atlassian.

Available on the [Atlassian Marketplace](https://marketplace.atlassian.com/).

---

## Why it exists

Most Confluence Mermaid apps save your diagram as a small raster image. Zoom in and it turns to
mush. Crisp renders real vector graphics, so a diagram stays sharp at 25% or 400%.

- **True SVG output** — never a rasterised screenshot
- **Full page width** — no artificial size ceiling
- **Click to zoom, drag to pan, open full screen**
- **Live preview while you type**, with syntax errors reported by line
- **Proper dark mode** that follows your Confluence theme
- **`classDef` and styling directives work** as written
- **Download as SVG or PNG**
- Every Mermaid diagram type — flowchart, sequence, class, state, ER, Gantt, C4, journey, mindmap,
  timeline, quadrant, block, XY chart

## No account. Ever.

Some Mermaid apps require every person on your team to create and log into a third-party account
before they can draw a box. Crisp doesn't. Install it and it works, for everyone, immediately.

## Your data never leaves Atlassian

Crisp carries Atlassian's **Runs on Atlassian** badge. It uses only Atlassian-hosted compute and
storage and makes **zero external network requests**. Diagram source is stored in the macro on your
own page.

You don't have to take our word for it — open your browser's DevTools Network tab and watch. There
is no request. Disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the diagram still renders and
zooms, because everything runs in your browser.
